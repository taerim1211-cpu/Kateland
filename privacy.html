Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

What we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, company (if given), the service you're interested in, and your message. This information is sent to us through Formspree, a third-party form processing service, and delivered to our inbox at info@katelandinc.com.

We do not use cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels on this site. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry about a project, consulting engagement, or investment opportunity
  • To keep a record of prospective client and partner conversations
  • To follow up about services you've asked about

How long we keep it

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain a record of business communications, typically no more than 24 months unless an active engagement continues.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete information you've submitted, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing info@katelandinc.com.

Third parties

Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree, which is subject to its own privacy policy. This site is hosted as a static site and does not run server-side code beyond form delivery.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we'll update this page and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@katelandinc.com.
