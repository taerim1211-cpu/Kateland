About Kateland INC

Builders and investors, working as one team.

We exist to help great ideas become great products — and great products become great companies.

Our Story

Founded on the belief that technology and capital should move together.

Kateland INC started as a small team of engineers and consultants building websites and applications for growing businesses. As our clients scaled, we saw an opportunity to do more than build software — to help fund and guide the companies we believed in.

Today, Kateland INC operates across five disciplines: web development, web applications, mobile applications, IT consulting, and investment. Each one informs the other, giving our clients and portfolio companies an unusually complete partner.

Mission

To build reliable, thoughtful technology and to back the people and companies building the future.

Vision

A world where great technical execution and smart capital are never out of reach for ambitious teams.
